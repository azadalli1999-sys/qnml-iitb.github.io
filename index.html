We study how atomic order gives rise to macroscopic behavior.

QNML — the Quantum Nanomaterial Lab — investigates emergent phenomena in quantum and correlated-electron materials — from synthesis and structural characterization to transport and spectroscopy at cryogenic temperatures.

Research

01 — active programs

Research interests: Quantum/Nano Material FabricationDefects2D MaterialHalide Perovskites OptoelectricalSensorsand other nano devices

FabricationLow-dimensional

Quantum and nanomaterial fabrication

We synthesize low-dimensional materials — from 2D layered crystals to 1D nanowires and 0D quantum dots — using furnace-based, sputtering, and wet-chemical routes, and integrate them into functional devices.

Defects2D Materials

Defect engineering in 2D materials

We study how vacancies, dopants, and grain boundaries in graphene and transition-metal dichalcogenides govern carrier mobility and electronic behavior, toward controlled defect engineering for device applications.

Halide PerovskitesOptoelectronics

Halide perovskite optoelectronics

We investigate perovskite nanocrystals and thin films for light-emission and photodetection, using PL and UV-Vis spectroscopy to connect defect states and composition to optoelectronic performance.

SensorsNanodevices

Sensors and other nanodevices

Building on our fabrication and characterization capabilities, we design nanoscale sensing and interconnect devices, evaluating their electrical and electrochemical response for gas, chemical, and biosensing applications.

Facilities

02 — instrumentation

End-to-end infrastructure spanning synthesis, thin-film deposition, and electrical, structural, and optical characterization — combining in-house instruments with shared access to the Nanolab facility.

Synthesis

Single-Zone Furnace with Gas Line

Tube furnace with integrated gas flow for inert or reactive atmospheres — used for annealing, thermal treatment, and CVD-type synthesis with precise temperature control.

Wet Bench Station

Analytical balance, probe and bath sonicators, hot air oven, and centrifuge for solution preparation, dispersion, cleaning, and drying ahead of characterization.

Thin-Film & Device Fabrication

Custom-Built Sputtering System

In-house DC/RF sputtering system for depositing metal and oxide thin films, used for electrode, contact, and functional-layer deposition.

Thermal Deposition System

Four-source resistive evaporation system for sequential or co-deposition of electrode contacts and multilayer stacks, with substrate rotator and temperature control for uniform films.

Electrical & Electrochemical Characterization

Janis Cryogenic Probe Station — 11 K

CH-202FF

Closed-cycle, cryogen-free cryostat with turbomolecular pumping, multiple micromanipulated probes, and a vibration-isolated stage for on-wafer testing down to 11 K. Optional IR/VUV/X-ray windows support spectroscopic measurements.

Janis 4-Probe Station — 77 K

RT-UHT-1

Liquid-nitrogen-cooled station with four independent micromanipulator probes for multi-terminal I–V, Hall effect, and temperature-dependent transport measurements.

Custom-Built Probe Station

Room-temperature station housed in a Faraday cage, giving a low-noise, EMI-shielded environment for high-impedance device I–V characterization.

Autolab Cyclic Voltammeter

Potentiostat/galvanostat for cyclic and linear-sweep voltammetry with a three-electrode cell — used for redox behavior and sensor or battery-material studies.

LCR Meter

Measures inductance, capacitance, and resistance, including frequency-dependent impedance and capacitance–voltage measurements under AC or DC bias.

Structural & Optical Characterization

FEG-SEM

Nanolab facility

Field-emission-gun scanning electron microscope for high-resolution nanoscale surface and microstructure imaging.

UV-Vis Spectrophotometer

Nanolab facility

Measures optical absorbance and transmittance for optical bandgap estimation and solution concentration analysis.

Photoluminescence Spectrometer

Nanolab facility

Probes light emission upon photoexcitation to determine optical bandgap, emission properties, and defect states.
